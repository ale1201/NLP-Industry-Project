# AI Disclosure

During this project, we used AI assistants (e.g., Claude) in a supporting capacity:

- **Code review and testing:** to help validate parts of our implementation, spot bugs, and assist in writing/checking unit tests.
- **Writing support:** to review and improve the clarity, grammar, and phrasing of the technical report and documentation.

All core ideas, methodology, code, and results are our own work. AI assistance was limited to review and polishing, not the generation of core content. Also, any AI generated part was review and checked carefully.
